Why URLovingly is different

Most invitation tools send everyone the same card. We built ours around the guest.

1

An envelope addressed to them

An animated envelope arrives with the guest's own name on it. Choose from four arrival styles — velvet, rose gold, botanical or celestial.

2

Scratch to reveal the date

Guests scratch a card to uncover the date, then a live countdown begins. Text appears letter by letter, like a handwritten note.

3

Multi-day selective event routing

Invite each guest only to the ceremonies that concern them — Haldi for family, the reception for colleagues. Nobody sees what they were not invited to.

4

UPI Shagun built in

Let guests send their blessings with a single UPI tap, right from their invitation page.

5

Live RSVP dashboard

Guests reply from their own link. You watch Yes / Maybe / No and the head-count update in real time — no more chasing messages.

6

Your own background audio

Upload the song that means something to you. It plays softly when the guest opens the invitation, with a mute button.

Questions

Yes. You sign in with Google using your Gmail address; we never see your Google password.

One payment covers one celebration — for example one wedding — including all its guest links, up to your plan's limit.

What happens when hosting ends?

After the hosting period (7 days, 6 months or 1 year, depending on the plan) the guest links show a friendly "expired" page.
